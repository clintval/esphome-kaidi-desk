Kaidi Desk Installer

Put the firmware on an assembled esphome-kaidi-desk board and join it to your Wi-Fi. It takes about two minutes.

Not yet tested on the assembled board. Nobody has built one yet. It has been flashed and joined to Wi-Fi on a bare ESP32-C3, with no desk attached.

This browser can't reach USB devices. Use Chrome or Edge on a computer.

This page needs to be opened over HTTPS.

Steps

  1. Plug the board's USB-C into your computer with a data cable. The port is isolated, so the desk can stay connected.
  2. Press the button above and choose the port, usually "USB JTAG/serial debug unit".
  3. Install, then enter your Wi-Fi name and password when asked.
  4. In Home Assistant, accept the discovered device within 15 minutes. Home Assistant sets its encryption key then; if you miss the window, unplug the board and plug it back in.

If it doesn't connect

Hold BOOT on the board, press RESET, then let go of BOOT and try again.

Good to know

  • Only for the assembled board. The protoboard build uses different pins.
  • Home Assistant offers new versions as an update; each one restarts the board, so install it while the desk is still.
  • It transmits at reduced Wi-Fi power, which is easier on the desk's 5 V supply, and some ESP32-C3 boards can't connect at full power. Keep it within good range of your router.
  • To change Wi-Fi later, come back here with the USB cable.
